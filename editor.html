Glyph editor

Edit the parametric centreline of one glyph of Emerald Rounded Game over its Pixel Vector original. Points are in source pixels (baseline y=12, cap top y=3, x-height y=6, pixel centres at .5). Saved files go to data/glyph_overrides/ in the font project.

Polyline: click to add points, Enter or double-click to finish, Esc cancels. Select: drag points, click a point to edit its radius (joints are sharp and mitred unless given a radius or a round join), Delete removes it. Arc: click centre, then start point, then end point. Circle: centre then a point on it. Dot: click. Cut mask: click polygon corners, Enter finishes; the red area is subtracted from the finished outline (good for trimming joins flush with the cap or baseline; "Clip to rows" does that for the whole glyph). Fill: click polygon corners, Enter finishes; the polygon is solid ink with no stroke width (the arrows are drawn this way). Any selected stroke can also be edited as raw JSON under its properties.
